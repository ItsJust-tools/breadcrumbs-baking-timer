## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added

- **Breadcrumbs Baking Timer (v1.0.0)**: First release — step-by-step multi-timer for home bakers with parallel countdowns, per-step audio/notification alarms with dismissible flash, mid-bake step reordering/duplication/deletion that preserves elapsed time, browser persistence of running state, curated preset plans (bread, pizza, croissants, banana bread), shareable plan URLs, and a live timeline of overlapping steps.
